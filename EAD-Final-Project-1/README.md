# Beauty Salon Management System

Welcome to the Beauty Salon Management System! Here’s what you can expect from this project:

- **User-Friendly Booking**: Effortlessly book appointments at your favorite beauty salons and choose from a wide variety of services.
  
- **Salon and Service Listings**: Browse through a curated list of available salons and view the specific services each one offers.

- **Appointment Management**: Enjoy full control over your bookings with the ability to create, read, update, and delete your appointments.

- **Admin Roles**:
  - **General Admin**: Responsible for adding and removing salons from the platform and authorizing their listings.
  - **Salon Owner Admin**: Can manage their salon’s services, view scheduled appointments, and update any information related to their salon.

This system is designed to enhance the beauty booking experience for both customers and salon owners, making it easier than ever to connect and enjoy your favorite services. Dive in and start managing your beauty appointments today!
